## Brewing information

Recipes, water and salt suggestions, pH targets, bitterness and colour estimates, and hop flavour charts are calculated estimates and suggestions only. Check them against your own equipment, ingredients and judgement before you brew, and follow safe practices with hot liquids, chemicals and cleaning products. Hop flavour scores come from hop suppliers' published descriptions through the HopDatabase project and may be incomplete or wrong.
